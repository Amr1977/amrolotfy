Background

Engineer & Founder

I'm a software engineer with a QA automation background — Java, Selenium, TestNG — who transitioned into full-stack product development and Islamic tech entrepreneurship.

My products are Arabic-first, designed for Muslim communities globally. I hold an ISTQB Foundation certification and have worked at companies including Incorta and BadrIT, and spent years building iOS apps for Saudi Arabia's aviation authority (GACA) and consumer fintech.

I believe technology can be a form of worship — every line of code in a product that helps someone recite Quran or feed their neighbor is a continuous charity (صدقة جارية).
